    What's New
    Here's what's new ✦
    OMEN Hub was just updated. Here's a look at everything that's new, improved, and fixed.
    v4.2.1
    March 15, 2026
    Installed
    What's New
    Browse the entire store without leaving your flow. Every section now has a "View All" button that opens a full browsing experience — filter by genre, price, or sale status to find exactly what you're looking for.
    Your hub, your colors. The accent color picker now includes a custom slot — dial in any color with the wheel or type a hex code directly. Your pick saves automatically.
    See your gear while you configure it. The mouse and headset panels in Peripherals now show a large product image side-by-side with your settings, so you always know exactly what you're tuning.
    Improvements
    My Devices now shows real product photos for each device in your setup, with one-tap shortcuts to jump straight to the right configuration tab.
    The navigation bar now subtly frosted when you scroll — keeping it readable no matter what's behind it.
    Fixes & Stability
    Tapping "Configure" on a peripheral card now takes you straight to that device's tab — no more landing on Keyboard every time.
    A handful of edge cases causing the dashboard to become unresponsive have been resolved.
    Security
    This update includes important security patches. We recommend keeping OMEN Hub up to date to stay protected.
    v4.1.0
    February 3, 2026
    Previous
    What's New
    OMEN AI just got smarter. A completely rebuilt engine means faster game detection, better recommendations, and presets tuned specifically for the titles you play most.
    Your screenshots, front and center. The new Gallery widget brings your captures right to your home dashboard — flip through them in slideshow mode or jump to a full view.
    Profiles that follow every detail. Game Profiles now remember your fan curves and lighting scenes per title — switch games and your whole setup switches with you.
    Improvements
    Performance stats refresh up to 4× more often for a real-time feel during intense sessions.
    Fan curves now transition more smoothly between speeds — no more audible stepping under load.
    Fixes & Stability
    Undervolting settings no longer reset when you close and reopen the panel.
    Lighting preview flicker on mode changes has been eliminated.
    System Cleaner totals now reflect your selections accurately in real time.
    v4.0.2
    January 12, 2026
    Previous
    Fixes & Stability
    Fixed a startup crash that could occur when no HyperX peripherals are connected.
    Accent color theme choices now persist reliably between sessions.
    Widget layout no longer breaks on high-resolution displays set to 200% scaling or above.
    Security
    Includes a security patch addressing a vulnerability in the OMEN driver interface. Updating is strongly recommended.
